## Brand & Style
This design system orchestrates a dualistic, high-concept cinematic journey tracing the metaphysical shift from existential abyss to radiant transcendence. Designed for an ultra-luxury anime editorial and media curation ecosystem, the aesthetic bridges dark romanticism with sublime golden-hour optimism.

### Visual Style Spectrum
The core architecture operates along a directional spectrum:
1. **The Eclipse/Abyss Zone (Darkness):** Pitch charcoal, smoked carbon, and glowing blood-crimson embers. Evokes danger, intense narrative weight, solitary anti-heroes, and raw combat tension.
2. **The Twilight/Liminal Axis (Transition):** Deep cosmic obsidian, midnight indigo, violet-slate, and diffused silver starlight. Evokes mystery, psychological shifts, and cosmic scale.
3. **The Elysium/Sunrise Zone (Light):** Amber gold, incandescent champagne, and luminous alabaster cream. Evokes transcendence, quiet resolution, and timeless triumph.

The interface unites high-fashion editorial rigor with cinematic atmospheric depth: razor-thin luminous hairline borders, dual-mode frosted glassmorphism, vertical Japanese typography anchors, atmospheric chromatic gradients, and sweeping negative space.

## Colors
The color palette behaves not as static UI tokens, but as a living horizontal chromatic narrative.

### Palette Architecture
- **Pitch Obsidian (Neutral Base):** `#050507` — Infinite depth canvas for the dark state, grounding all high-contrast chromatic flares.
- **Blood Ember (Primary Accent):** `#e62332` (supported by deep crimson `#8a0c16`) — The pulse of conflict, visceral actions, critical tags, active triggers, and abyss-tier telemetry.
- **Radiant Amber (Secondary Accent):** `#fbbf24` (supported by burnished gold `#d97706`) — The manifestation of dawn, rare artifact badges, enlightenment states, and luminous focal points.
- **Violet Slate & Twilight (Tertiary Atmosphere):** `#1e1b38` to `#3b3558` with muted starlight silver `#94a3b8` — Liminal neutral bridge connecting pitch shadow to luminous daylight.
- **Elysian Cream (Luminous Surface Anchor):** `#fdfbf7` with warm champagne glow `#fef3c7` — The foundation for pristine light-zone surfaces, editorial parchment sections, and inverted daytime experiences.

### Gradients & Chromatic Application
- **Eclipse Horizon:** Linear transition from `#050507` via `#1e1b38` across to `#2a1728` with `#e62332` ember reflections.
- **Elysian Dawn:** Linear transition from `#3b3558` through `#d97706` into `#fef3c7` and `#fdfbf7`.
- **Atmospheric Glows:** Radial overlays strictly capped at 15–25% opacity with 80px+ Gaussian blurs to emulate cinematic spotlighting and particle halos without compromising textual legibility.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances classical dramatic authority with immaculate high-tech editorial precision.

- **Display & Headline Roles (EB Garamond):** Evokes the lineage of high-end literary publications, archival art books, and high-fashion title sequences. It carries regal dignity in both all-caps tracked-out titles and italicized poetic epigraphs.
- **Body & Functional Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Grounded, razor-sharp modern geometric sans with geometric balance. Provides clinical legibility in complex UI, data dashboards, character metadata, and streaming specifications.
- **Editorial Tracking Rules:** All uppercase metadata (`label-md`, `label-xs`) mandates wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.22em`), evoking museum exhibition labels and anime production ledger stamps.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid 12-column editorial grid configured to accommodate both horizontal scroll journeys (representing the journey from Darkness on the left to Light on the right) and vertical responsive scrollytelling.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile` (&lt; 768px): 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Single-axis vertical stacking with collapsible contextual panels.
  - `Tablet` (768px – 1024px): 8 columns, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Split-screen juxtaposition (Darkness counterpart pinned beside Light counterpart).
  - `Desktop / Wide Cinema` (&gt; 1024px): 12 columns, max content envelope `1560px`, `margin-lg` (4rem), `gutter-lg` (2.5rem). Expansive multi-tier widescreen layout with ample editorial negative space.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a modular scale rooted in 4px base units. Generous exterior padding around hero features allows character key art to bleed into background glows without suffocating textual content.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of translucent physical stratification, optical luminosity, and frosted refraction.

### Atmospheric Glassmorphic Surfaces
- **Tier 0 (Void Ground):** Pure flat `#050507` or Elysian `#fdfbf7` background.
- **Tier 1 (Atmospheric Base):** Translucent backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`), layered with `rgba(18, 16, 28, 0.65)` in dark domains or `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.7)` in light domains.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Curated Artifacts):** Elevated modal/card panels with dual inner glow:
  - Darkness Zone: Hairline border `1px solid rgba(230, 35, 50, 0.18)` and top inner sheen `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Light Zone: Hairline border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)` with ambient shadow `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.

### Chromatic Light Flares & Hairlines
Edges are sculpted with razor-thin gradient strokes (`border-image: linear-gradient(to right, rgba(230, 35, 50, 0.4), rgba(251, 191, 24, 0.4)) 1`). Visual emphasis is established when a hover interaction triggers an interior ember bloom: a diffused 20px edge glow transitioning from `#8a0c16` to `#fbbf24`.

## Shapes
The shape language favors razor-sharp architectural restraint with softened corners (Level 1: Soft). 

- **Primary Geometry:** Base UI cards, dialogue frames, and buttons adhere strictly to `0.25rem` (4px), lending an artisanal, print-editorial quality reminiscent of collector manga slipcases and animation cel cels.
- **Outer Shells & Modals:** Large containment windows utilize `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Contrasting Badges & Pills:** Micro-labels, episode timecodes, and state indicators may leverage true capsules (`9999px`) to create intentional geometric tension against the dominant rectilinear grid.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Abyss Primary (Dark Trigger):** Obsidian black background, 1px border `rgba(230, 35, 50, 0.5)`, text in `#fdfbf7`. Hover: Background shifts to `#8a0c16` with a diffused blood-crimson outer halo (`0 0 20px rgba(230, 35, 50, 0.35)`).
- **Dawn Primary (Light Trigger):** Amber gold `#d97706` background with sharp text in `#050507`. Hover: Scaled luminosity to `#fbbf24` with champagne starlight shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(251, 191, 24, 0.35)`).
- **Ghost Editorial:** Transparent fill, tracked uppercase font (`label-md`), bottom hairline border only. Transitions to full chromatic gradient underline on interaction.

### Frosted Editorial Cards
- Multi-layered glass cards with high-definition backdrop filters.
- Top section holds poetic meta-indices (`ACT I: THE HOLLOW`, `VOLUME 04 // REFLECTION`).
- Hover shifts card perspective with a slight 3D elevation and illuminates the respective regional color tone (crimson edge on dark panels, warm amber on light panels).

### Metadata Chips & Status Emblems
- Compact 20px height capsules with ultra-subtle borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Prefix iconography replaced with miniature Japanese character indicators or geometric glyphs (e.g., `✦` for Celestial/Dawn, `▲` for Crimson/Abyss).

### Inputs & Search Fields
- Monolithic rectangular bars with low-opacity dark obsidian backgrounds (`rgba(5, 5, 7, 0.8)`).
- Active focus state: Replaces border with an electric gradient line shifting from crimson ember into starlight silver, coupled with EB Garamond floating placeholders.

### Narrative Chapter Tabs & Navigation
- Segmented strip tracking along the dualistic spectrum.
- Active tab indicator is not a solid block, but an illuminated, glowing horizontal razor-slit (1px height) resting underneath the typographic label.

### Japanese Poetic Vignette Banners
- Specialized component featuring vertical text writing modes (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) flanking wide cinematic stills, punctuated by tiny golden kanji watermarks and serial sequence numbering.
