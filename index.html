✦ THE MANIFESTO
“To look into the abyss of human cruelty is not to succumb to it, but to discover the quiet, unbreakable courage required to say: I have no enemies.”

The Darkness: Eren's Burden

In Eren Yeager's world, cruelty is systemic, predatory, and ancient. Freedom is not an innate gift but a prize that must be violently seized against a hostile universe. His trajectory illustrates the terrifying cost of determinism: when one fights monsters with monsters, the boundary between salvation and annihilation dissolves.

The Light: Thorfinn's Transcendence

Thorfinn walked the identical road of vengeance, bathing his youth in bloodshed, only to discover its complete spiritual bankruptcy. His greatness does not lie in slaying warriors, but in laying down his blade to till the soil, enduring humiliation with dignity, and demonstrating that the highest valor is mercy.
